TIMBERBORN — LIVE OAK + SPANISH MOSS
Art brief for Eromrab's Tidetails mod

WHAT THIS IS
A tree for the city-builder Timberborn. The look is stylized and chunky
(see folder 2-game-style), not realistic. Think vanilla oak/mangrove, but
the silhouette of a Florida southern live oak draped in Spanish moss.

Folders in this pack:
  1-target-look          Real trees. Match THIS silhouette and moss, not the photo detail.
  2-game-style           Vanilla Timberborn oak. Match THIS art style and scale.
  3-current-placeholder  What is in the game now. Do NOT copy this moss.
  4-meshes-to-replace    Current .timbermesh files (scale/slot reference only).


TARGET LOOK
- Southern live oak (Quercus virginiana): wide, flat, spreading crown.
  Heavy limbs reach out sideways more than they go up.
- Spanish moss: gray-green curtains hanging FROM the branches, especially
  the outer skirt. The top of the crown stays mostly green. Moss is not
  dots, mushrooms, flowers, or icicles sitting on the leaves.
- Seedling: little or no moss.
- Dry mature: drought-stressed leaves, moss can stay.
- Dead mature: bare snag. Moss optional / sparse.
- Stump: short leftover after cutting.


SCALE
- Ground footprint: 1 x 1 tile. Height: 3 tiles. A tile is 1 x 1 x 1.
- Origin at the TILE CORNER (0,0,0). Trunk centered at (0.5, 0, 0.5).
- Y is up.
- Canopy MAY overhang neighboring tiles (vanilla oak already does).
- Keep the trunk in the center of the tile so cutting still works.
- Grid in the preview PNGs is 1 tile per square.

Vanilla mature oak is only ~170 triangles. You can go higher, but keep it
stylized and cheap — a few thousand tris on the mature tree including moss
is plenty. This is not a Cities: Skylines tree.

MATERIALS (exact names on the meshes)
  Tidetails.LiveOak         trunk + leaves
  Tidetails.SpanishMoss     hanging moss only, double-sided

First pass: no custom textures needed. UV to those two in-game materials.
Custom PNG textures are optional later.


EXPORT
Timberborn uses a custom format, .timbermesh, via Mechanistry's Blender plugin:
  https://github.com/mechanistry/timbermesh

Environment / tree settings:
  - Merge meshes: ON
  - Vertex animation: OFF
  - No animation clips

If you cannot export .timbermesh, send a .blend (or FBX) with:
  - Y-up, origin at tile corner, trunk at (0.5, 0, 0.5)
  - Material names as above
  - One object (or clearly named objects) per stage listed above

Do not include colliders, icons, or game JSON. We will wire those in.


DO NOT
- Put round leaf-cards of moss on TOP of the canopy (reads as mushrooms).
- Make a few thin white drips (the current placeholder).
- Copy Cities: Skylines / photogrammetry trees. Wrong style, and we cannot
  ship other people's assets.


CONTACT / ITERATION
Mature alive first, then the other stages. Screenshots or a turnaround
of LiveOakMature is enough for a first review.

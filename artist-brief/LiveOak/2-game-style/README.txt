Vanilla Timberborn oak. Match this art style, scale, and chunky canopy —
not the real photos in folder 1.

The grid is 1 tile per square. The tree sits on a 1x1 tile and is 3 tiles tall.
Canopy may overhang neighbors.

OakMature_vanilla.timbermesh is the in-game mesh if you have the Timbermesh
Blender plugin and want to inspect it.
